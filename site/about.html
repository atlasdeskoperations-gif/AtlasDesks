Who we are

Operators, not vendors.

Atlas Desks is a small AI-operations studio. We do not sell software and walk away — we install a desk inside your business and run it, month after month, until it is boring.

  • One desk per function, added one at a time. We say no to "AI runs the company" on day one — we get there desk by desk.
  • A human approves anything that touches money, contracts or customers.
  • You keep everything — prompts, workflows, data, exports.

What we believe

Most AI projects fail on operations, not models. The work is in the hand-offs: who checks what, what gets logged, what happens when the model is wrong. That is the part we own.

Our advantage is not the model. It is the workflow, the approval rules and the monthly review that make a desk trustworthy enough to hand real work to.
